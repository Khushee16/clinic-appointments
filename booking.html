Book your appointment in two minutes.

Pick a treatment, a dentist, a date and a time slot. On a PHP host this form saves your booking and returns a confirmation reference; on this static demo it shows a clearly labelled demo state instead.

How booking works
  1. Choose a treatment and dentist above.
  2. Pick a date and an available time slot.
  3. Submit — on a PHP host, appointment.php validates your details, saves the booking to data/appointments.json and returns a confirmation reference.
  4. On this static demo, you'll see a clearly labelled demo confirmation instead — nothing is saved.
